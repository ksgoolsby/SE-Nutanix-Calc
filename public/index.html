    Read this before you present it. Illustrative simulation, not a performance model: timings are not to scale, and placement is simplified (real AOS spreads extent groups across all nodes and disks, and reserves capacity for rebuild). Rebuild speed in practice depends on node count, network, disk type, and free capacity. Rebuild needs enough free space across the surviving nodes to hold the lost copies (size for N+1). Check specifics against the Nutanix documentation for the AOS version you are quoting.
